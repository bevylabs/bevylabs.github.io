Privacy Policy for SERU Prep

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Introduction

SERU Prep (listed on Google Play as "SERU Mock Test: PCO Licence") is a study app developed by BevyLabs that helps London private hire drivers prepare for the SERU assessment. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

SERU Prep is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Transport for London (TfL).

1. Information We Collect

SERU Prep does not collect or send any personal information to BevyLabs or to anyone else. There is no account or login, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking in the app.

Your answers, progress, mock test results and settings (such as the language you choose for explanations) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded.

2. Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play gives us limited order details (such as an order number, date, country and price) so that we can manage purchases and refunds. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

To restore a purchase on a new device, the app asks Google Play which purchases belong to your Google account. This check happens between your device and Google Play.

3. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your progress at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

4. Children

SERU Prep is intended for adults preparing for a private hire driver's licence (applicants must be 21 or over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, for example because we add a new feature, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
