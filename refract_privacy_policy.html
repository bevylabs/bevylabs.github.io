Privacy Policy for Refract

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Refract is a mixed reality puzzle game by BevyLabs for Meta Quest headsets. You bend beams of light with mirrors and prisms on a board that sits on your real table. This policy explains what the game uses on your headset and what it sends to us: nothing.

1. We do not collect your data

Refract does not collect, store or send any personal data to BevyLabs. The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not talk to any server of ours.

2. Microphone (optional)

After you solve a puzzle you can blow on a glowing dandelion. The first time this happens, the game asks for microphone access so it can notice when you blow. While you play, it only checks how loud the last fraction of a second was. Sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the microphone is switched off when you leave the game.

You can refuse microphone access, or turn it off later in your headset's settings, and wave your hand through the dandelion instead.

3. Your room and your hands

  • Space and scene data: the game uses the room setup on your headset (walls, tables and other surfaces) to place the board on your table and to bounce light off your walls. This stays on your headset; we never see it.
  • Hand tracking: the game follows your hands so you can pick up and move pieces. Hand movements are used on your headset only and are not saved or sent anywhere.

4. What is saved on your headset

Refract saves which puzzles you have solved, the puzzle you were on, your comfort settings (light brightness and background sound), and the crystals and stars you leave around your room. All of this is saved only on your headset.

5. Meta

When the game starts, it asks Meta's platform whether your Meta account owns Refract, so that the game only runs for people who bought it. This check happens between your headset and Meta. Meta may also collect data when you use its store and headset; see the Meta Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

  • Spatial data: to find your table and walls (asked when the game starts).
  • Hand tracking: to play with your hands. Controllers also work.
  • Microphone: only for blowing on the dandelion (asked the first time it appears). Optional.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Because everything stays on your headset, you are in control of it. Uninstalling Refract deletes your saved progress, settings and keepsakes. We have nothing to delete on our side, because we never receive anything.

8. Children

Refract does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Meta Quest headsets and accounts have their own age requirements, set by Meta.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the game ever needs to collect anything, it will ask you first.
