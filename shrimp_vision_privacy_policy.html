Privacy Policy for Shrimp Vision

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Shrimp Vision is an app by BevyLabs that counts shrimp seed (PL) from a photo. This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone, what it sends to us, and why.

1. Counting happens on your phone

The app counts PL on your phone. Counting works without internet. Your photos, counts, notes, weights and hatchery name are stored on your phone.

2. "Help improve counting" (optional)

The app asks once whether you want to help make counting more accurate. This is off unless you say yes, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Help improve counting. If it is on, the app sends us, only when your phone is on Wi-Fi:

  • Your PL photos (the copy the app keeps, at most 2048 pixels wide).
  • The boxes the app drew and the boxes you added or removed.
  • The counts, your hand count if you entered one, and the sample and batch weights.
  • The app and model version, and your phone's language and region setting (for example "en_IN").

We never send your hatchery or farm name, your notes, your location or your contacts. Photos are only sent for counts made after you said yes.

We use these photos and boxes only to train and test the counting model, so the app counts more accurately. We do not sell them or share them with anyone else, and we do not use them for advertising.

3. Questions in the app (optional)

The app may ask a few questions, such as whether you work at a hatchery or a farm, your country, how PL is priced and whether a bonus is given. Every question can be skipped. We use the answers only to decide what to build next.

4. Free trial and subscription

Shrimp Vision has a 30-day free trial. After that, a subscription through Google Play is needed to count new photos; your old counts stay free to view and share.

So that the trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app, the app makes a scrambled code from your phone's Android ID (an ID Android gives this app on your phone) and sends it to Google Firebase with the date your trial started. The raw Android ID never leaves your phone. We use this code only to run the trial and any free access we give you, and to stop misuse of the trial.

Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not see your card, UPI or bank details; the app only checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active.

5. No account, no tracking

You do not need an account. The app signs in to Google Firebase with an anonymous ID that is not linked to your name, email or phone number. The app has no ads and no analytics.

6. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Storage, Cloud Firestore, anonymous Authentication) stores the trial record and what you choose to send: photos in Google's United States region, and boxes, counts and answers in Google's Singapore region. See Firebase privacy and security.

7. Permissions

  • Camera: to take a photo of PL to count.
  • Photos / gallery: to choose an existing photo to count.
  • Internet: to check the free trial and subscription, and to send photos and answers you chose to share. Counting itself works offline.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and check the subscription.

8. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your phone: counts and photos stay until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.
  • Shared photos and answers: we keep them while we use them to improve counting, and for at most 5 years.
  • Trial record: the scrambled phone code and trial dates are kept for as long as the app is offered, so the trial stays one per phone.
  • Deleting what you sent: turn off Help improve counting, then email us the Sharing ID shown in Settings. We will delete everything sent with that ID within 30 days.

9. Children

Shrimp Vision is a work tool for shrimp hatcheries and farms. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we start collecting anything new, the app will ask you first.
