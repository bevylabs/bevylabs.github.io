Privacy Policy for SPSV Prep

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Introduction

SPSV Prep (listed on Google Play as "SPSV Test Ireland: Taxi Prep") is a study app developed by BevyLabs that helps people in Ireland prepare for the SPSV Driver Entry Test for taxi, hackney and limousine drivers. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

SPSV Prep is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the National Transport Authority (NTA).

1. Information We Collect

SPSV Prep does not collect or send any personal information to BevyLabs or to anyone else. There is no account or login, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking in the app.

Your answers, progress, mock test results, test date and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded.

If you choose "Report a mistake" on a question, the app opens your own email app with a message to us that includes the question and the reason you picked. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use it only to fix the question and to reply to you.

2. Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play gives us limited order details (such as an order number, date, country and price) so that we can manage purchases and refunds. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

To restore a purchase on a new device, the app asks Google Play which purchases belong to your Google account. This check happens between your device and Google Play.

3. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your progress at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete your emails at the address below.

4. Your Rights

Because the app keeps your data on your device and we hold no personal data from it, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you have the rights given by the GDPR over that correspondence, and you can contact the Data Protection Commission in Ireland if you have a concern.

5. Children

SPSV Prep is intended for adults preparing for an SPSV driver licence. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

6. Map Data

Some Dublin Area Knowledge questions are based on map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. The map data is built into the app; the app does not contact OpenStreetMap or share your location.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, for example because we add a new feature, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
