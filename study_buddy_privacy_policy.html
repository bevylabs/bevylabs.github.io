Privacy Policy for Study Buddy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Study Buddy, an educational application developed by BevyLabs. This privacy policy explains BevyLabs' commitment to protecting your privacy while using our app, Study Buddy.

1. Information We Collect

While our app strives to minimize data collection, certain features require communicating with trusted third-party service providers (Google Vertex AI and Google AdMob):

  • Content of Images: When you use the feature to generate practice questions, the image and text content extracted from study notes you capture or select is processed via Google Cloud Vertex AI solely to generate relevant educational questions and answers.
  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: We use Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded video ads that unlock bonus scans. Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate IP location, and ad performance data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and maintain compliance.
  • Technical and Diagnostic Data: Basic device information (device model, operating system version, and technical performance metrics) may be collected to ensure app compatibility and stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide Core App Functionality: Processing educational text and notes to generate multiple-choice and essay practice questions.
  • Rewarded Advertisements: Serving short rewarded video advertisements when requested by the user to grant bonus scan quotas.
  • App Improvement and Stability: Monitoring compatibility issues, preventing crashes, and maintaining service quality.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share necessary information only with the following trusted third parties:

  • Google Cloud & Vertex AI: Used to analyze study note images and generate questions. Learn more in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display rewarded video ads. For information on how Google manages advertising data, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third parties.

4. Use of App Permissions

Study Buddy requires the following permissions:

  • Camera access: To capture images of educational text and study notes.
  • Gallery / Storage access: To select existing images of educational text from your device.
  • Internet access: To communicate with Google Cloud Vertex AI for question generation and to load rewarded video ads.

5. Advertising & User Choice

Study Buddy includes optional rewarded video ads. You can control or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time through your device settings:

Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed to be safe for learners of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our advertisements are delivered via Google AdMob adhering to Google's Families and Content policies, featuring family-safe, non-intrusive rewarded ads that users explicitly choose to watch.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BevyLabs may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on our privacy policy page linked in the Google Play Store listing. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
