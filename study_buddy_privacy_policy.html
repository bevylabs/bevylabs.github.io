Privacy Policy for Study Buddy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Study Buddy, an educational application developed by BevyLabs. This privacy policy explains BevyLabs' commitment to protecting your privacy while using our app, Study Buddy.

1. Information We Collect

While our app strives to minimize data collection, certain features require communicating with trusted third-party service providers (Anthropic, Google Cloud Functions, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play and Google Fonts):

  • Content of Images: When you use the feature to generate practice questions, the photos of study notes you capture or select are sent through our server on Google Cloud Functions to Anthropic's Claude AI, solely to generate educational questions and answers. The photos are not stored on our server.
  • Answers You Type: When you check essay answers, your answers and the quiz's reference answers are sent the same way to Anthropic's Claude AI to be graded. The same applies when you ask why an answer is wrong. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • Question Reports: If you report a question, the question text, the reason you choose and any comment you write are sent to BevyLabs through Google Forms so we can review problems with AI-generated content. Reports do not include your name or device identifiers.
  • Age Check: On first launch we ask for your birth month and year. Only your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) is stored, on your device. Your birth date is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: We use Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded video ads that unlock bonus scans. Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate IP location, and ad performance data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and maintain compliance. For users aged 13 to 17, ad requests are marked as coming from someone under the age of consent, so Google shows only non-personalised ads, with no remarketing, and only content rated PG or lower. Users under 13 are not shown ads.
  • Device Verification: Before an AI request is accepted, Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm the request comes from a genuine copy of the app installed from Google Play. This sends a short-lived token, not your personal details.
  • App Usage (Analytics): If you are 18 or older, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used: for example, when you pass the age check, add note pages, create a quiz, reach the daily scan limit, watch a rewarded ad or finish a quiz. Each event comes with basic device information (device model, operating system, app version, approximate country from your IP address), an app instance identifier and, where available, the Android Advertising ID. We do not send your notes, questions, answers or birth date to Analytics. No analytics data is collected before the age check or from anyone under 18. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, analytics is only collected if you agree to it in the consent form shown when you first use the app (storing information on your device and audience research); you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy settings" in the app's Settings.
  • Purchases: If you subscribe to Buddy Plus, the purchase is handled by Google Play, which processes your payment details. BevyLabs never sees your card or other payment details. We can see order details in Google Play Console, such as the order ID, product, price, date and the buyer's email address, and use them only for support and refunds.
  • Fonts: The app downloads its font from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the font is fetched. No notes, answers or other study content are included.
  • Technical and Diagnostic Data: Basic device information (device model, operating system version, and technical performance metrics) may be collected to ensure app compatibility and stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide Core App Functionality: Processing educational text and notes to generate multiple-choice and essay practice questions, and grading essay answers.
  • Safety and Quality: Reviewing reported questions to improve the quality and safety of AI-generated content.
  • Rewarded Advertisements: Serving short rewarded video advertisements when requested by the user to grant bonus scan quotas.
  • Buddy Plus: Checking with Google Play whether your subscription is active, so the app can unlock its perks.
  • Understanding How the App Is Used: Seeing, in total, how many people complete each step (for example, how many create their first quiz) so we can find and fix the parts of the app where people get stuck.
  • App Improvement and Stability: Monitoring compatibility issues, preventing crashes, and maintaining service quality.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share necessary information only with the following trusted third parties:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Used to read study note photos, generate questions and grade answers. Anthropic does not use data sent through its API to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Cloud Functions and Firebase App Check: Used to run the small server that passes AI requests to Anthropic, and to verify the app. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display rewarded video ads. For information on how Google manages advertising data, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to measure app usage as described above. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Forms: Used to receive question reports you choose to send.
  • Google Play: Used to process Buddy Plus subscriptions and payments. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Fonts: Used to download the app's font. See: https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third parties.

4. Use of App Permissions

Study Buddy requires the following permissions:

  • Camera access: To capture images of educational text and study notes.
  • Gallery / Storage access: To select existing images of educational text from your device.
  • Internet access: To send note photos and answers to the AI service for question generation and grading, to load rewarded video ads, to send app usage analytics, to download the app's font and to check Buddy Plus subscriptions.
  • Notifications: To send an optional daily study reminder to help keep your study streak going.
  • Google Play Billing: To let you buy and manage a Buddy Plus subscription.

5. Advertising & User Choice

Study Buddy includes optional rewarded video ads. You can control or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time through your device settings:

Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings in the app's Settings. Users aged 13 to 17 are only shown non-personalised ads.

6. Age Requirement and Children's Privacy

Study Buddy is intended for users aged 13 and over. We design the app to follow children's privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the GDPR, and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code:

  • Under 13: The app is not directed at children under 13. Users who indicate they are under 13 cannot use the AI features, are not shown ads and are not included in analytics; they can only study decks created earlier. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Ages 13 to 17: Only non-personalised ads (PG or lower), no app usage analytics, and AI content held to rules that keep it suitable for a 13-year-old.
  • AI disclosure: Questions, answers and feedback are written by an AI system, not a person. Each question is marked "AI" and can be reported with the flag button.
  • No personal details: The AI is instructed never to ask for or repeat personal details, and the app has no chat, profiles or sharing with other users.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Storage and Retention

  • On your device: Your saved decks, quiz results, study streaks, Buddy's coins and room items, focus session stats, your Buddy Plus status, settings and the note photos kept for open-book review are stored only on your device. Note photos are deleted when you delete the deck, and all app data is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Subscriptions: Google Play keeps the record of your Buddy Plus subscription, so it can be restored after reinstalling the app or on a new phone. Coins and room items are not restored, because they are stored only on your device.
  • Sent for processing: Note photos and answers are passed through our server without being stored, and are used by Anthropic only to fulfil your request, under Anthropic's commercial terms and data retention rules. Our server logs only technical details, such as the type of request and its size, never the content.
  • Analytics: App usage events are kept by Google Analytics for at most 14 months and then deleted automatically. They are used only in total, to understand how the app is used, and are never used to identify you.
  • Reports: Question reports are kept only as long as needed to review them. You can ask us to delete a report by emailing us.

8. Buddy Plus Subscription

Buddy Plus is an optional subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in Google Play under Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BevyLabs may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on our privacy policy page linked in the Google Play Store listing. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
